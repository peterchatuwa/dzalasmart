The farmer's season

Every field is tracked through 8 real-life stages

Nothing here is manual paperwork. Each stage below updates itself — triggered by a warehouse scan, a text message, a satellite pass, or a weighbridge reading — so everyone downstream sees the same live status.

STAGE 1
Input Redeemed
A farmer collects seed or fertiliser and the depot scans their voucher token.
STAGE 2
Land Prep
Updates when a tractor is booked or a farmer texts that the field is ready.
STAGE 3
Planting
Farmer sends the sowing date by phone; the system predicts the harvest window.
STAGE 4
Vegetative Growth
Satellites re-check every 5 days for crop stress, no site visit needed.
STAGE 5
Harvesting
Farmer reports total bags harvested through the same *413# menu.
STAGE 6
Post-Harvest
Warehouse staff test grain moisture with a handheld meter before accepting it.
STAGE 7
Marketing
Weighbridge logs the sale and pays the farmer straight to mobile money.
STAGE 8
Income Logged
Profit is calculated automatically and feeds the farmer's credit history.

Trust and reliability

Built for patchy networks and sensitive data

📶

Works without signal

Field devices save their work on the phone or tablet first. Once signal returns, records quietly sync in the background — nothing is lost if a village has no coverage that day.

🔒

Personal details stay locked

A farmer's name and phone number are scrambled (encrypted) in storage, and only unlocked for staff who are allowed to see them — the same standard banks use for account details.

🏛️

Talks to existing government systems

Connects to the existing subsidy voucher system (FISP/AIP) and the national market price system (NAMIS) automatically, so nobody has to check eligibility or prices by hand.

⚡

Stays fast at national scale

Even during the busiest harvest weeks, look-ups for national reports are built to answer in under 40 milliseconds — faster than a blink.

Why we built this

The farmer's cry — and what we do about it

The real problem is rarely lack of information. It's uncertainty — uncertain rain, uncertain prices, uncertain inputs, uncertain payment, uncertain help.

💬

"I need inputs on time, at a price I can afford."

A live supplier price book with the landed cost into your district, so you see the real delivered price before you order.

💬

"I need to know if this season will fail before I waste money."

District intelligence — rainfall, soil and risk for every district — feeds straight into your yield and profit numbers.

💬

"I need to sell at a fair price and get paid."

Market and gate prices sit next to your budget, and every delivery and payment is tracked against the buyer.

💬

"I need help when drought, pests or disease hit."

Alerts on your fields and one "Get help now" button that opens Nzeru za Alimi AI with your farm context already loaded.

💬

"I need to know what today's work actually is."

Your season plan turns into dated tasks and shows only what's due, late, or blocking money — not a task backlog.

💬

"I need someone to believe my farm is bankable."

A Farmer Passport, scored from your own verified records — the evidence a lender or buyer asks for.

Getting there

A three-phase rollout, not a single big-bang launch

Phase 1

Connect to what already exists

Link up with the government's existing voucher and price systems first, so no active program is interrupted.

Goal: data flows in and out without a single hiccup to current systems.
Phase 2

Pilot with real cooperatives

Field teams map real plots by GPS and test the offline sync with two regions: Lilongwe and Mzuzu.

Goal: 5,000 farmers onboarded and the first digital warehouse receipts issued.
Phase 3

Open it up nationwide

Switch on the Farmers Union's oversight tools and reporting for funders, across all 28 districts.

Goal: nationwide crop tracking and automatic credit for certified farmers.
